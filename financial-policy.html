Financial & payment policy

Transparent, simple and for your peace of mind. Please review our financial and payment policy and our prepaid package agreement before your visit.

Payment

Payment is due in full at the time of service, unless prior financing arrangements have been approved.

Credit card processing fee

A 3% processing fee applies to all credit card transactions.

This fee covers our payment processing cost and is added to your total at checkout.

Financing options

We accept CareCredit and Cherry financing, with flexible payment plans for qualified patients, including plans as low as 0% interest for 6 months. Any financing fees, interest or program charges are separate from your procedure fee and are set by CareCredit or Cherry.

Appointments & cancellations

We value your time and ours. Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel your appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee.

Consultations & deposits

The photo assessment and the visit with our Patient Care Coordinator are free. The consultation with Dr. Thomas is $75, applied to your treatment and forfeited for a no-call, no-show. Endolift® dates can be secured with a $250 deposit, applied to your treatment.

No refunds

All completed aesthetic treatments, services and product purchases are final and nonrefundable.

Prepaid packages are subject to their signed terms. See the Prepaid Treatment Package Agreement below.

Prepaid packages

Prepaid Treatment Package Agreement

TermsValidityRefundsYour commitment

This agreement outlines the terms for prepaid treatment packages purchased at LIBRE Aesthetics & Wellness.

Package validity

All prepaid treatment packages must be completed within twelve (12) months of the original purchase date. Unused treatments after this period are forfeited, subject to applicable law and financing-provider requirements.

Refunds & medical exceptions

Package purchases are final and nonrefundable. If a documented medical contraindication prevents completion of the remaining treatments, LIBRE may review the unused portion for an appropriate extension, alternative treatment or credit, at the discretion of the treating physician.

Qualifying medical reasons

A medical review may be considered for serious, documented conditions that make continued treatment unsafe or medically inappropriate, such as:

  • Pregnancy
  • New or worsening serious medical condition
  • Cancer diagnosis or active treatment
  • Severe allergic reaction or treatment complication
  • Physician-documented contraindication

Package use & changes

  • Packages are valid only for the original patient and are nontransferable, unless approved in writing by LIBRE.
  • Any changes to the treatment plan must be medically appropriate and approved by the treating provider.
  • Completed treatments are not refundable and may be valued at their regular individual prices if a partial credit is approved.

Financing

Packages purchased through CareCredit or Cherry are subject to the terms and conditions of the financing provider, including treatment-delivery timeframes and refund requirements, which take precedence where applicable.

Patient acknowledgment

Patients who buy a prepaid package sign this agreement at purchase, acknowledging that they have read, understood and agree to the LIBRE Aesthetics & Wellness Prepaid Treatment Package Agreement and the terms above.
