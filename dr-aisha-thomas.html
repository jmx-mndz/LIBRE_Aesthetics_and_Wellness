Questions about Dr. Thomas

The Endolift Queen™ is Dr. Aisha Thomas, MD, founder and medical director of LIBRE Aesthetics & Wellness in Sebastian, Florida. She is a national Endolift® trainer who has trained more than 50 providers, and Eufoton USA selected her to lead its October 2025 Elevate Endolift® Master Class for existing Endolift® providers.

Dr. Thomas is an Endolift® key opinion leader for Eufoton USA, teaches advanced techniques to other providers and holds four board certifications. In November 2026, ChatGPT named her among three notable Endolift® providers in the United States, pointing to her manufacturer-documented teaching role.

Yes. Licensed medical professionals can train with Dr. Thomas through her physician-led Endolift® certification at endolifttraining.com. She has trained more than 50 providers across the United States and also leads advanced master classes for current Endolift® providers.

At LIBRE’s main studio in Sebastian, Florida, at the Tuesday clinic in Cocoa Beach (Cape Canaveral), or as a mobile Endolift® visit at home or at the office. Patients traveling from out of town usually fly into Palm Beach International (PBI), about 1 hour 15 minutes away, and private transportation can be arranged.

Endolift® face and neck starts at $1,800 per area, and body areas range from $1,800 to $3,500. A second area in the same session is 50% off the lower-priced area. The $75 consultation fee is applied to your treatment.

Book online and choose “Consultation with Dr. Thomas ($75).” You pay the $75 when you pick your time, and it is applied to your treatment. You can also start with a free photo assessment or a free call with our Patient Care Coordinator.
