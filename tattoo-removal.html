How it works

Shatter. Clear. Fade.

  1. 01

    Ultra-short pulses

    Enlighten III® delivers laser energy in trillionths of a second, targeting the ink while protecting the surrounding skin.

  2. 02

    Ink shatters into dust

    The pulse breaks ink into tiny particles — far smaller than older lasers can — so it clears more efficiently.

  3. 03

    Your body clears it

    Over the following weeks your immune system naturally removes the particles, and the tattoo fades session by session.

Good to know

Tattoo removal questions

Tattoo removal is performed by Dr. Sydney Nichols, MD, using the Enlighten III® picosecond laser.

Most tattoos need several sessions, spaced weeks apart so your body can clear the ink between treatments. The number depends on the size, colors, ink depth, age of the tattoo and your skin type. Dr. Nichols gives you an estimate at your consultation.

Enlighten III® treats all ink colors. Black and dark inks usually fade fastest; some colors can take more sessions.

Most patients describe a quick snapping sensation. The area can be cooled or numbed to keep you comfortable, and sessions are short.

Yes. Many patients do a few sessions to lighten a tattoo so their artist can create a better cover-up.

Yes — cosmetic tattoos such as brows and eyeliner can be treated after an assessment.

Keep the area clean, protected and out of the sun. Mild redness, swelling or blistering can happen and usually settles within days. You’ll receive full aftercare instructions after each session.

Pricing is by size: extra small $150, small $250, medium $350, large $500 and extra large from $750 per session, with savings on packages of six. Cosmetic eyebrow tattoos start at $250.
