The Healthy Hair Program

Sixteen weeks, step by step

Dr. Thomas’s program treats the scalp and the whole you, because hair is not just keratin. It reflects your inner health.

  1. Week 1

    Consultation & diagnosis

    We look for the cause: a questionnaire, your hair-loss stage, scalp photos, a hair pull test and labs when needed.

  2. Week 2

    Lab review & nutrition

    We review your labs, give an IV nutrient infusion when indicated, and start your supplements and scalp care.

  3. Week 3

    PRP for the scalp

    Your own platelet-rich plasma is placed close to the follicles to wake up the stem cells at the root.

  4. Weekly

    Laser & injections

    Red-light laser therapy for the follicles, biotin complex injections and regular scalp health checks.

  5. Week 12

    Progress check

    We assess your growth and decide whether another PRP session will help.

  6. Week 16

    Final review

    Labs compared with your baseline and a plan to maintain results. We reassess again at six months.

Your provider adjusts the plan to you. Prescription treatments only when medically indicated. Results vary.

Good to know

Hair restoration questions

Women and men with thinning hair or early hair loss usually see the best results. We start with a hair analysis to understand the cause, because nutrition, hormones, stress and genetics all play a role.

The program runs over about 16 weeks: a consultation and labs, a lab review with nutrition support, PRP for the scalp, then weekly visits with laser therapy and injections. We check your progress at week 12 and review your labs at the end. Full regrowth takes longer, so we reassess at six months.

We keep you comfortable with topical numbing, injection numbing or a nerve block for the scalp, so most patients feel only mild pressure. Because PRP comes from your own blood, allergic reactions are not expected, and you can return to your day afterward.

PRP works best when you still have fine, thin hairs, because it wakes up the stem cells at the root so they grow fuller again. Transplants are better for areas with no hair left. Your provider will tell you which fits you.

Hair grows slowly, so changes usually appear over several months. Results vary from person to person.
