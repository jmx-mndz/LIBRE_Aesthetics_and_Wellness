Your roadmap

Six months to build a new lifestyle

A structured, month-by-month journey with monthly provider follow-ups and ongoing accountability.

  1. Month 1

    Discover & Reset

    Assess your health, habits, body composition, motivations and goals. Create a personalized lifestyle prescription.

  2. Month 2

    Nourish

    Develop sustainable nutrition habits, improve food quality and learn practical meal planning.

  3. Month 3

    Move & Strengthen

    Build a realistic exercise routine emphasizing resistance training and muscle preservation.

  4. Month 4

    Restore & Rebalance

    Improve sleep, stress management, emotional eating patterns and daily routines.

  5. Month 5

    Build Independence

    Strengthen self-management skills and assess medication needs and progress.

  6. Month 6

    Thrive & Maintain

    Review progress, set your long-term maintenance plan and decide on medication continuation, adjustment or supervised discontinuation.
