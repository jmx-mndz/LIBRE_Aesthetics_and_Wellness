Frequently asked questions

Your physician assesses your goals, treatment areas, skin laxity and suitability. Refine focuses on firming and tightening, Sculpt on fat reduction and contouring, and Transform delivers our most dramatic non-surgical sculpting with Endolift®.

Endolift® body is priced per area, from $1,500 for a small localized fat deposit to $3,500 for full thighs. A second area in the same session is 50% off (on the lower-priced area), and signature packages bundle popular combinations from $3,200. See the full Endolift® price list for every area.

Paired areas count as one: both arms, both sides of the bra rolls, both flanks, both inner or outer thighs, both banana rolls and both knees. The full abdomen, upper back and buttocks are each one area.

Body contouring refines shape and targets stubborn areas; it is not a substitute for weight loss. For metabolic health and weight goals, explore the Lifestyle Reset — the two pair beautifully.

Most body treatments involve little to no downtime. Your provider reviews aftercare for your specific plan.

Some tightening can be noticeable early, but body contouring results continue to develop over the following weeks to months as your body responds and builds collagen.
