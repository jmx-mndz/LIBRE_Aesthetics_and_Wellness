Gold Incision® & Gluteal Harmonization questions

Deep cellulite dimples are caused by fibrous bands beneath the skin that pull the surface down. Gold Incision® precisely releases these bands, which is why it addresses a structural cause rather than only tightening the skin temporarily.

Gold Incision® is a minimally invasive technique developed in Brazil by aesthetic physician Dr. Roberto Chacur. Dr. Thomas is a Gold Incision® licensee.

Gold Incision® starts at $3,500 for a small area and includes 2 syringes of Radiesse®. Additional syringes are $600 each. Your final price depends on the number of dimples, the treatment area, the severity of cellulite and the complexity of correction.

Gluteal Harmonization starts at $2,500 for 100 mL of product. The total investment depends on the product selected, the volume required, your anatomy and your goals.

Options include hyaluronic acid, hyperdilute Radiesse® (which can last for years with maintenance), Bellafill® PMMA filler, or alloClae® — processed donor fat tissue offered through our partners, which can be a longer-lasting solution. Dr. Thomas recommends the right product for your goals at your consultation.
