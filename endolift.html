Introducing Endolift®

Surgical-level results, no incisions

Endolift® is our most requested treatment for loose skin, stubborn fat and the early signs of aging — and it is Dr. Thomas’ primary offering.

Many people are told that a real lift is only possible with surgery. Endolift® bridges that gap: a laser-based, minimally invasive treatment that tightens skin, restructures tissue and reduces small fat deposits, with many patients noticing a difference after a single session and no long, painful recovery.

  • Face & neck — under-eyes, brows, cheeks, jawline, jowls, double chin and neck
  • Body — arms, bra rolls, back, abdomen, flanks, thighs, knees and buttocks
  • Physician-performed — by Dr. Thomas or Dr. Nichols only

How Endolift® works

One fiber. Three effects.

  1. 01

    A hair-thin laser fiber

    After numbing, a micro-optical fiber as thin as a hair is placed just beneath the skin through a tiny entry point — no incisions and no scars.

  2. 02

    Precise 1470 nm energy

    Controlled laser energy gently heats the tissue, breaking down small pockets of stubborn fat exactly where they are needed.

  3. 03

    Tightening & new collagen

    The heat contracts the tissue for an immediate lift and switches on new collagen, so skin keeps getting firmer and smoother for weeks.

Before you proceed

Is Endolift® right for you?

A great fit if you want to

  • Tighten loose skin on the face, neck or body
  • Define the jawline, soften jowls or reduce a double chin
  • Reduce under-eye bags without surgery
  • Treat small pockets of stubborn fat that diet and exercise won’t shift
  • Avoid incisions, general anesthesia and long recovery

Not suitable if you have

  • Pregnancy
  • Active skin infections, wounds or skin conditions in the area
  • A history of blood clots (thrombosis or thrombophlebitis)
  • Severe liver or kidney disease
  • Uncontrolled high cholesterol or triglycerides
  • An organ transplant, long-term blood thinners or active cancer

Your physician reviews your full medical history before every treatment.

Endolift® is ideal for mild to moderate skin laxity and small, stubborn fat deposits on the face, neck or body. Your physician reviews your goals, anatomy and medical history at your consultation and will tell you honestly whether Endolift® — alone or combined with another treatment — is right for you.

Endolift® is performed by Dr. Aisha Thomas, a national Endolift® trainer who has trained more than 50 Endolift® providers in the United States, and by Dr. Sydney Nichols, who is certified in Endolift®.

Most patients find Endolift® comfortable. Local anesthesia is used when your physician recommends it, so you can relax during your session.

About 60 minutes for a face area and about 90 minutes for a body area. Most patients return to their normal routine right away.

Most patients need only one session and see a visible improvement right away, with results continuing to develop over the following weeks as new collagen forms. In some cases your physician may recommend an additional session.

Results are long-lasting — the tightening and collagen renewal can last for several years. Longevity varies with age, skin quality and lifestyle, and your physician will suggest a simple maintenance plan.

Complications are rare. Temporary swelling, redness, mild bruising or numbness in the treated area are the most common effects and usually settle within days. Your provider reviews aftercare before you leave.

Endolift® works beautifully on its own. For some goals Dr. Thomas may suggest pairing it with neurotoxin, filler, biostimulators or laser renewal for a complete, natural result.
