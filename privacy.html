Privacy policy

LIBRE Aesthetics & Wellness (“LIBRE,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, phone, email) when you book, inquire or chat with us.
  • Your treatment interests and appointment information.
  • Basic website analytics (pages visited, device type).

How we use it

  • To schedule and confirm appointments and respond to inquiries.
  • To send reminders, follow-ups and — with your consent — occasional offers.
  • To improve our website and services.

Protected health information

Medical information is handled in accordance with applicable laws, including HIPAA. Please do not send sensitive medical details through website chat or the inquiry form.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example scheduling and communication platforms) and as required by law.

SMS terms

By providing your phone number and opting in, you agree to receive text messages from LIBRE Aesthetics & Wellness, including appointment reminders, follow-ups and — if you separately opt in — occasional marketing messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Medical disclaimer

All treatments require a consultation and individual medical assessment. Results vary from person to person and are not guaranteed. Before-and-after images are shared with permission; representative images are labeled. Pricing is subject to change and to clinical evaluation. Content on this website is educational and does not replace professional medical advice.
